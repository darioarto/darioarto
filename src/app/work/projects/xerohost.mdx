## About XeroHost
XeroHost is a VPS and game server hosting provider and part of XeroGroup LLC, operating its own network (AS216102) with infrastructure in Miami, Dallas and São Paulo.

- Troubleshooting of support incidents for FiveM and Minecraft (Java and Bedrock), and development of a knowledge base for the support team.
- Marketing and communications, including email, Discord and Trustpilot campaigns.
