## Background
I founded the network in August 2023 with a clear goal: to start a new chapter, challenge myself and keep learning. I built it alongside friends such as Hugo, Aiden, Samuel and Thegrab, as well as other Staff members. Not everyone joined at the same time, but each of them played a key role in helping the project grow.

The early stages were not easy. We started with small investors and my own funding, which proved difficult to sustain. Once I was able to cover the costs myself for a few months, we moved to Tect.host, an affordable and reliable provider, for around two months. An investor later provided us with a VPS (Virtual Private Server), which we used for over three months until all of our server files and backups were deleted, forcing us to start again from scratch.

Shortly afterwards, Jimmy, zBlackDemon and Retroflas, the owners of BKCraft3D Network, proposed merging our servers. The merger worked for three to four months, until they decided to leave and we once again lost all of the network's files and configuration. Internal conflicts and the loss of players and Staff made that period especially challenging.

A few months later, Lucas Robles, CEO of XeroHost, offered us hosting with excellent specifications at a very competitive price. Since then, the network has been hosted on XeroHost with great results.

## My role
- Configuration of the entire server using my own knowledge and tooling.
- YAML configuration files and MySQL/MariaDB databases for permission and user management.
- Leadership of the development team, and management of the Staff team for a period, with custom guides and documentation.
