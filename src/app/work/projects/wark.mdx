## Wark: Moderation tool
Wark is our Discord application for moderation and the automation of actions.
It was created in 2023, the same year the company was founded, and has been continuously updated ever since to suit any type of server.
You can add it to your Discord server here:
https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=1185958479312191558

## WarkGuard: Security and protection
WarkGuard is our most complete and advanced application. It includes many of the moderation commands available in Wark, along with an Anti-Raid system.
Its standout feature is Verification, which lets members of your server verify themselves to gain access to the rest of the channels through a role.
You can try it yourself using the official link:
https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=1210290864140853289

## Reach
Since 2023, our applications have reached more than 160 servers combined, over three times the reach of most moderation and security applications.
